You are at the Finance Department with the detective. You have arranged a meeting with Dr. James Thornton, a postdoc who agreed to take a look at the formula on the USB drive.

- Alex Martin was attacked in his apartment three days ago — hit on the back of the head. He had a concussion and was hospitalized, but has since come round, been discharged, and is recovering at home. He feels better and can be visited in his apartment.

### The drive
- Tim attacked Alex to get this drive. He claimed he'd heard it held some kind of super-formula — one that supposedly lets you get rich on the stock market.
- Whether any of that is true is exactly what you need to find out. The USB drive needs expert analysis — that's why you're here. James Thornton has agreed to look at the formula on it.
