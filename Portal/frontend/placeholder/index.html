How it works

This research project explores different ways to learn English through detective stories. You will be placed in one of two groups: one works with an interactive AI chatbot, the other with reading and writing activities. Both groups follow the same story and the same mystery.

One episode per week, 4 weeks, about 40-60 minutes each. Everything is online - phone or computer.

Who can join

Adult learners (18+) with intermediate English (B1-B2). You can read and write in English, even if it is not always easy.
