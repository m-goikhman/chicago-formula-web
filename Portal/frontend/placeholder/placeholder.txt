How it works
This is a research project exploring different ways to learn English through detective stories. You'll be placed in one of two groups: one works with an interactive AI chatbot, the other with reading and writing activities. Both groups follow the same story and the same mystery.
One episode per week, 4 weeks, about 30–40 minutes each. Everything is online — phone or computer.
Who can join
Adult learners (18+) with intermediate English (B1–B2). You can read and write in English, even if it's not always easy.
