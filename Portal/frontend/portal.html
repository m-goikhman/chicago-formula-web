Try an AI detective for English learning

Building English Writing Confidence in Adult Learners through AI Text Detective Game

How it works

This research project explores different ways of learning English through detective stories. You will be assigned to one of two groups: one works with an interactive AI chatbot, the other with reading and writing activities. Both groups follow the same story and the same mystery.

Two episodes per week, 2 weeks, approximately 40–60 minutes each. All online — phone or computer.

Who can join

Adults (18+) with intermediate English (B1–B2). You can read and write in English, even if it is not always easy.

  • 2 weeks, 2 episodes/week, approx. 40–60 min each
  • All online — phone or computer
  • 280 SONA Time Credits for UniTn students

Scopo dello studio

Questo studio vuole esplorare come la lettura e scrittura supportate dagli strumenti basati su Intelligenza Artificiale possano aiutare chi studia l'inglese come lingua straniera. In particolare, vogliamo capire se questo tipo di attività può:

  • Rendere la pratica della scrittura in inglese più coinvolgente
  • Ridurre l'ansia che alcune persone provano quando scrivono in inglese

Lo studio è finanziato tramite una borsa di dottorato erogata da FBK (Fondazione Bruno Kessler), nell'ambito del programma congiunto con l'Università degli Studi di Trento.

Chi può partecipare

Stiamo cercando persone con queste caratteristiche:

  • Adulti (18+)
  • Livello di inglese intermedio (B1–B2)

Per partecipare avrai bisogno di un computer, smartphone o tablet con connessione internet.

Cosa ti chiederemo di fare

Se decidi di partecipare, ti chiederemo di svolgere un'attività che durerà complessivamente due settimane:

  1. Compilare un questionario iniziale (circa 10 minuti) sulle tue esperienze con l'inglese e su come ti senti quando scrivi in questa lingua.
  2. Svolgere attività di scrittura in inglese (circa 40–60 minuti per episodio, due episodi a settimana). Ti verrà assegnato casualmente uno di questi due gruppi:
    • Gruppo Gioco: giocherai a un gioco investigativo con personaggi AI con cui chattare per risolvere un mistero
    • Gruppo Lettura: leggerai un giallo in inglese, farai esercizi di lingua e risponderai a domande con l'aiuto di un tutor AI
  3. Tutte le attività si svolgeranno online (tramite applicazione web), e potrai farle in qualsiasi momento durante lo studio.
  4. Compilare brevi questionari (circa 5 minuti) dopo ogni episodio.
  5. Compilare un questionario finale (circa 10 minuti) sulla tua esperienza alla fine delle due settimane.
  6. Partecipare a un'intervista opzionale (circa 20–30 minuti, online) in cui potrai raccontarci più nel dettaglio cosa hai pensato dell'attività. L'intervista è completamente facoltativa.

In totale, lo studio richiederà circa 4–5 ore distribuite nell'arco di due settimane, più eventualmente 20–30 minuti per l'intervista.

Possibili rischi e benefici

Rischi: Non prevediamo rischi significativi. Alcune persone potrebbero sentirsi leggermente in ansia mentre scrivono in inglese, ma questo fa parte della normale esperienza di apprendimento. Puoi interrompere in qualsiasi momento se ti senti a disagio.

Benefici: Partecipando, avrai l'opportunità di praticare l'inglese scritto in un contesto nuovo e senza giudizio e provare strumenti educativi innovativi con intelligenza artificiale. Agli studenti UniTn saranno attribuiti 280 SONA Time Credits.

We invite you to participate in a research study conducted by the University of Trento and the Fondazione Bruno Kessler. Before deciding whether to participate, please read this document carefully and contact us if you have any questions.

Study purpose

This study aims to explore how AI-supported reading and writing activities can help adults learning English as a foreign language. In particular, we want to understand whether this type of activity can:

  • Make English writing practice more engaging
  • Reduce the anxiety that some people experience when writing in English

The study is funded through a doctoral fellowship awarded by FBK (Fondazione Bruno Kessler), as part of its joint programme with the University of Trento.

Who can participate

We are looking for people with the following characteristics:

  • Adults (18+)
  • Intermediate English level (B1–B2)

To participate you will need a computer, smartphone, or tablet with an internet connection.

What we will ask you to do

If you decide to participate, we will ask you to complete an activity lasting two weeks in total:

  1. Complete an initial questionnaire (approximately 10 minutes) about your experiences with English and how you feel about writing in this language.
  2. Complete English writing activities (approximately 40–60 minutes per episode, two episodes per week). You will be randomly assigned to one of two groups:
    • Game group: you will play a detective game with AI characters you can chat with to solve a mystery
    • Reading group: you will read a detective story in English, complete language exercises, and answer questions with the help of an AI tutor
  3. All activities will take place online (via a web application), and you can complete them at any time during the study period.
  4. Complete brief questionnaires (approximately 5 minutes) after each episode.
  5. Complete a final questionnaire (approximately 10 minutes) about your experience at the end of the two weeks.
  6. Participate in an optional interview (approximately 20–30 minutes, online) where you can tell us in more detail what you thought of the activity. The interview is entirely voluntary.

In total, the study will require approximately 4–5 hours spread over two weeks, plus optionally 20–30 minutes for the interview.

Possible risks and benefits

Risks: We do not anticipate significant risks. Some people may feel slightly anxious when writing in English, but this is part of the normal learning experience. You can stop at any time if you feel uncomfortable.

Benefits: By participating, you will have the opportunity to practise written English in a new, judgement-free context and try innovative AI-based educational tools. University of Trento students will receive 280 SONA Time Credits.

Voluntary participation

Your participation is entirely voluntary. You have the right to:

  • Refuse to participate without giving reasons.
  • Withdraw from the study at any time without penalty.
  • Decline to answer specific questions or complete specific exercises if you do not wish to.

Privacy and data processing

Your personal data will be processed in accordance with the GDPR (EU Regulation 2016/679) and Italian privacy legislation.

Data we will collect:

  • General information (age, gender, native language, English level)
  • Your responses to questionnaires
  • The texts you write during the activity
  • Optionally, an audio recording of the interview (only if you consent)

If you do NOT participate in the optional interview: your participation will be entirely anonymous. You will access the study via a personal alphanumeric code without providing your name, email, or other contact details. We will not know who you are.

If you participate in the optional interview: we will ask you to provide your contact details to arrange the interview. Your participant code will be linked to your contact details, but this information will remain strictly confidential and will not be shared with anyone outside the research team.

Data retention:

  • Contact details (if provided for the interview) will be deleted at the end of the study
  • Questionnaire responses and game logs will be anonymised
  • Anonymised data will be retained for as long as needed for research and scientific publications

Your rights: to access your personal data, request correction or deletion, object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time.

Use of results

The results of this study will be used to understand how AI can support language learning, to publish scientific articles and conference presentations, and to improve the design of educational tools. All published results will be anonymous.
