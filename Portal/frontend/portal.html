Building English Writing Confidence in Adult Learners through AI Text Detective Game

Before you participate

Please read both documents and confirm your consent.

This study explores how AI-supported detective story activities can help adult English learners improve writing confidence and reduce writing anxiety.

Participation is voluntary and takes about 4-5 total hours over four weeks. You can withdraw at any time without penalty.

The full study information sheet is provided by the research team before participation.

Data is processed in line with GDPR and used only for research purposes. Published results are anonymized and cannot identify participants.

Contact details are requested only for participants who opt into the optional interview and are removed after the study.
