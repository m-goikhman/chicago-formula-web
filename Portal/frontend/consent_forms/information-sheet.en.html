Building English Writing Confidence in Adult Learners through AI Text Detective Game

Study on writing confidence in English for adults through an AI detective game

We invite you to participate in a research study conducted by the University of Trento and the Fondazione Bruno Kessler. Before deciding whether to participate, please read this document carefully and contact us if you have any questions.

Study purpose

This study aims to explore how AI-supported reading and writing activities can help adults learning English as a foreign language. In particular, we want to understand whether this type of activity can:

  • Make English writing practice more engaging
  • Reduce the anxiety that some people experience when writing in English

The study is funded through a doctoral fellowship awarded by FBK (Fondazione Bruno Kessler), as part of its joint programme with the University of Trento.

Who can participate

We are looking for people with the following characteristics:

  • Adults (18+)
  • Intermediate English level (B1–B2, no certificate required)

To participate you will need a computer, smartphone, or tablet with an internet connection.

What we will ask you to do

If you decide to participate, we will ask you to complete an activity lasting two weeks in total:

  1. Complete an initial questionnaire (approximately 10 minutes) about your experiences with English and how you feel about writing in this language + take a short vocabulary test (approximately 5 min).
  2. Complete four English writing activities over two weeks (approximately one hour per episode, two episodes per week). You will be randomly assigned to one of two groups:
    • Game group: you will play a detective game with AI characters you can chat with to solve a mystery
    • Reading group: you will read a detective story in English, complete language exercises, and answer questions with the help of an AI tutor
  3. All activities will take place online (via a web application), and you can complete them at any time during the study period.
  4. Complete brief questionnaires (approximately 5 minutes) after each episode.
  5. Complete a final questionnaire (approximately 10 minutes) about your experience at the end of the two weeks + take a short vocabulary test (approximately 5 min).
  6. Participate in an optional interview (approximately 20–30 minutes, online) where you can tell us in more detail what you thought of the activity. The interview is entirely voluntary.

In total, the study will require approximately 4–5 hours spread over two weeks, plus optionally 20–30 minutes for the interview.

Possible risks and benefits

Risks: We do not anticipate significant risks. Some people may feel slightly anxious when writing in English, but this is part of the normal learning experience. You can stop at any time if you feel uncomfortable.

Benefits: By participating, you will have the opportunity to practise written English in a new, judgement-free context and try innovative AI-based educational tools. Voluntary participation

Your participation is entirely voluntary. You have the right to:

  • Refuse to participate without giving reasons.
  • Withdraw from the study at any time without penalty.
  • Decline to answer specific questions or complete specific exercises if you do not wish to.

Privacy and data processing

Your personal data will be processed in accordance with the GDPR (EU Regulation 2016/679) and Italian privacy legislation.

Data we will collect:

  • General information (age, gender, native language, English level)
  • Your responses to questionnaires
  • The texts you write during the activity
  • Optionally, an audio recording of the interview (only if you consent)

If you do NOT participate in the optional interview: your participation will be entirely anonymous. You will access the study via a personal alphanumeric code without providing your name, email, or other contact details. We will not know who you are.

If you participate in the optional interview: we will ask you to provide your contact details to arrange the interview. Your participant code will be linked to your contact details, but this information will remain strictly confidential and will not be shared with anyone outside the research team.

Data retention:

  • Contact details (if provided for the interview) will be deleted at the end of the study
  • Questionnaire responses and game logs will be anonymised
  • Anonymised data will be retained for as long as needed for research and scientific publications

Your rights: to access your personal data, request correction or deletion, object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time.

Use of results

The results of this study will be used to understand how AI can support language learning, to publish scientific articles and conference presentations, and to improve the design of educational tools. All published results will be anonymous.
