Dear participant, we wish to inform you that current data protection legislation, with particular regard to the scientific research context, establishes every person's right to the protection of their personal data. Data protection officer

Purpose and legal basis for processing

Personal data is processed in order to pursue the scientific purposes described in the participant information sheet for the following research project: Building English Writing Confidence in Adult Learners through AI Text Detective Game.

Common personal data is processed pursuant to Art. 6(1)(e) GDPR (performance of a task carried out in the public interest). The project is filed and retained for five years from 31/10/2026 at the Department of Psychology and Cognitive Science of the University of Trento.

Categories of personal data processed

The following personal data will be processed in the course of the project:

  • Demographic and contact data
  • Voice (audio recording of the optional interview, subject to consent)
  • Access and usage logs of the web application
  • Personal opinions expressed (only during the optional interview, if linked to identifying data)
  • Any special category data (health, racial/ethnic origin, political opinions, etc.) to the extent that they arise spontaneously during the interview

Processing methods

Processing is carried out using computerised tools, in particular: data analysis platforms (Python, Google Sheets), the study web portal for the initial screening questionnaire (background and self-rated proficiency), and questionnaire applications (e.g. Google Forms) where used for follow-up surveys. Security measures include: pseudonymisation for information collected during the optional interview, protected storage, and encryption.

Participants who do not take part in the personal interview are identified exclusively via a participant code that cannot be traced back to the individual. Email addresses collected during pre-registration are used solely to send the study link and are in no way linked to participant codes.

The only exception applies to participants who consent to the interview: in this case, the code will be temporarily linked to the email address provided (pseudonymised form) to enable contact. At the end of the research, all contact data and audio recordings will be permanently deleted.

Data retention period

At the end of the project, audio recordings of interviews and contact data will be deleted. Questionnaire responses, web-app logs, and pseudonymised interview transcripts will be retained for five years for research purposes.

Nature of data provision

Providing data is essential for the realisation of the research project; failure to provide data makes participation impossible.

Recipients of data

Personal data will not be communicated to third parties and will not be transferred outside the European Economic Area (EEA).

Dissemination of results

Statistical and/or scientific results (scientific articles, databases, conferences) will be disseminated only in anonymous and/or aggregate form, in a manner that does not allow identification of the data subject.

Data subject rights

Data subjects have the right to request from the Controller, at any time: access to their personal data, rectification, supplementation and, where the legal conditions are met, erasure, restriction of processing, and the right to object (Arts. 15 et seq. GDPR). Data subjects have the right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing. It is possible to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority pursuant to Art. 77 GDPR.
