Tim Kane. PhD student in Finance. He shares an office with Alex at the university. He looks almost as shaken as Fiona. He speaks fast, and it seems like he is trying not to cry.

"When did you arrive?"

"I left the university around six. Six, six-fifteen, somewhere around there. I had to — I needed to sort some things out. Personal stuff." He pauses.

"Yes — no — I mean, yes." He exhales. "Someone left something like that on my desk at work. A few days ago. I don't know who. I thought it was some stupid joke." He looks up. "Is it — do you think the same person could have attacked Alex?"
