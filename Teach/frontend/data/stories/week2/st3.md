Alone with Nina, Pauline sits down and takes a breath.

"You're Alex's business partner?" Nina asks.

"Yes. We've been working together for two years. He developed a financial formula. I use it for trading."

"And tonight?"

"We had an argument — earlier this evening. I can't continue using the formula without understanding how it works. He promised to explain it to me long ago but now he just keeps putting it off! Tonight was the same." She pauses. "He sent me to his university office to collect a USB drive. The formula is on it. I brought it to him, gave him back his keys, and left. That was around twenty to seven."
