The address is a cheap motel by the road. Room 14.

Nina knocks. After a long moment, the door opens.

Alex stands there, alone. No kidnapper. No danger. Just a tired, frightened man who has not slept well in days. His head hurts — he left his medicine at home.

For a moment, nobody speaks. Then Alex lets out a slow breath. He almost looks relieved.

"I knew someone would come," he says quietly. "I just didn't know what to do."
