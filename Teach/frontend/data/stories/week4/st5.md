Nina shows Susan a photo of the formula from Alex's USB drive — the formula Tim attacked him to steal.

Susan looks at it. For a second, she almost laughs. Then her face changes.

"This isn't real," she says quietly. "It looks like mathematics, but it means nothing. It's just symbols put together to look clever."

"Could it be a mistake?" Nina asks.

"No. Someone made this to fool people." Susan shakes her head. "For two years Alex told me he was close to something big. I believed him. Maybe this was it — a lie, the whole time."

She is silent for a moment. "I keep asking myself if I should have helped him more."
