Next, Nina visits Pauline Thompson. Pauline is sharp and careful. She chooses her words slowly.

"Alex and I are old friends," she says. "We grew up together. Later we became business partners."

"What kind of business?" Nina asks.

"Trading. Alex had a special formula for the stock market, and I used it to make trades." Pauline pauses. "It worked. We made a lot of money."

Nina asks about the night of the party. Pauline says Alex asked her to bring a USB drive from his office — the one shaped like a little airplane. She brought exactly that one. "He never said it was the wrong one," she adds firmly.

"Lately I had been asking him to explain how the formula works," she says. "He always changed the subject. I never understood why."
