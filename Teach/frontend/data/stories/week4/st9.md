Nina's phone rings. It is the station.

"Alex's phone is on again," her colleague says. "We have a location — a town about twenty kilometres from here."

Nina thinks fast. The phone being on does not prove much. Maybe Alex turned it on. Maybe someone else did. Maybe he was taken; maybe he walked away on his own.

There is only one way to find out.

"I'm on my way," she says. "We go in quietly, and we look around first."
