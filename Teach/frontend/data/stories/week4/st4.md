Nina drives to the university. Professor Susan Nakamura meets her in a quiet office. The professor looks tired.

"I keep thinking about Alex," Susan says. "First the attack, and now this."

Nina asks about Alex's studies. Susan is quiet for a moment.

"There is something you should know," she says. "Alex is not a student here anymore. He failed an important exam — twice. We had to let him go in October."

Nina is surprised. "Did people know?"

"No. I don't think he told anyone." Susan looks down. "But he kept coming to the building, as if nothing had changed. I felt sorry for him, so I said nothing."
