It is December 23rd, two days before Christmas. Sergeant Nina Reyes is at her desk when a young woman walks into the police station. She is pale and afraid.

"Detective? I'm Fiona — Alex's girlfriend." Her voice shakes. "I think Alex has been kidnapped."

Alex Martin was attacked at his own party five days ago. Tim Kane is already in a cell for it, and the case is closed.

But now Alex is gone.
