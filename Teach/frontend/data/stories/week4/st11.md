Nina sits down. Slowly, in pieces, Alex tells the truth.

There is no formula, he says. He made it up two years ago — symbols that looked brilliant but meant nothing. The money was real, but it came from Pauline's skill, not from his formula. She never knew.

And there was more. He failed out of his PhD program in October. He told no one — not Pauline, not Fiona. He was too ashamed.

"Everything was falling apart," he says. "The police had the drive. Pauline kept asking questions. I couldn't face any of it. So I ran."

He looks at Nina. "I didn't have a plan. I never did."

Nobody took Alex Martin. He ran from the truth — and from himself.
