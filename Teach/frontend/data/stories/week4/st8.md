"I have to tell you something," Nina says. "We asked an expert to check the formula. It isn't real. It's nonsense — just symbols that look clever."

Pauline stares at her. "That's impossible. A fake formula doesn't make millions for two years."

"The money was real," Nina says. "But maybe the formula never did anything."

Pauline is quiet for a long time. Slowly, her face changes. "He always avoided my questions," she says. "Now I understand. There was nothing to explain."

Then another thought hits her. "If the formula did nothing... then the money was me. I read the market. I chose every trade. It was me, the whole time."

Her voice is hard now — anger and pride together. "He lied to me for two years. And if Alex got scared, I know exactly what he did. He ran."
