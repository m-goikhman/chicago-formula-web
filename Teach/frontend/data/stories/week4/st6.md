That evening, Nina finds Ronnie Snapper at a quiet bar. He is calm and well-dressed, a drink in front of him. He is leaving the city tomorrow, he says, for "family business."

Nina asks if he knows where Alex is.

"No idea," Ronnie says. "And before you ask — I didn't take him. Why would I? Alex always paid me back, on time, with interest. A man like that is good for business."

He smiles a little. "Tim was the problem, not Alex. And Tim is already in a cell."

Nina watches him. His story is simple, and it makes sense. The idea that Ronnie kidnapped Alex begins to fall apart.

"If you want," Ronnie adds, "I can ask around. I know people who notice things. I don't like loose ends."
