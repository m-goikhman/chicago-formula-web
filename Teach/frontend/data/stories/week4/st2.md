Nina gives Fiona a glass of water and asks her to start from the beginning.

"Yesterday morning we had breakfast together," Fiona says. "He was sad and quiet, but he was there. Then I went out to buy Christmas presents. When I came back at three, the apartment was empty."

She lists what she found. Alex's phone is off. Some clothes and one bag are gone. But his medicine — the medicine for his head injury — is still on the table. There is no note. The door was locked, and nothing is broken.

"He would never leave without his medicine," Fiona says. "And he never turns his phone off. Someone took him. I think it was Ronnie — the man he owed money to."
