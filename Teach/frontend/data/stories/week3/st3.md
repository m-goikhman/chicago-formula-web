The phone on Nina's desk rings. Her boss wants to see her.
He asks about Ronnie Snapper, one of the witnesses in Alex Martin's case. The police have watched the Snapper family for years. Everyone is sure they are not honest, but no one has ever proved it.

And now Ronnie Snapper is part of the story. Nina remembers the loan: a large part of the money Alex and Pauline used to start trading came from Ronnie.

"Look into Alex's business with Ronnie," the lieutenant says. "Where the money came from, where it went. Maybe this time we find something."
